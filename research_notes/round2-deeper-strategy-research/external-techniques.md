# External techniques for Round 2: permanent-impact jumps and online control

## 1. Which latent-state and jump mechanisms are worth adding?

### Takeaway

The strongest missing mechanism is an **online, two-channel posterior for a permanent level shift in S**, driven primarily by offset-corrected new limit prices and secondarily by IOC-implied S. Use it to reset the latent state and then keep continuous volatility separate from jump risk; do not let one large jump inflate a volatility scalar for minutes and disable profitable jump-following trades.

### Cited Findings

- **Ryan Prescott Adams and David J. C. MacKay, “Bayesian Online Changepoint Detection” (2007), arXiv:0710.3742, DOI 10.48550/arXiv.0710.3742.** The paper derives an online message-passing distribution over the current run length and deliberately separates the changepoint prior from the modular observation model. It supports maintaining a posterior over regime changes online, including predictive probability that a new run has begun; it does **not** supply a trading rule or a high-frequency LOB calibration. [Original arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.3742)
- **Matias Altamirano, François-Xavier Briol and Jeremias Knoblauch, “Robust and Scalable Bayesian Online Changepoint Detection,” ICML 2023, PMLR 202:642–663; arXiv:2302.04759.** This generalized-Bayes method obtains robust conjugate posteriors with closed-form algebraic updates using diffusion score matching. It supports the specific idea that robust online changepoint inference need not mean particle filtering or a deep model. The authors’ speed comparison is for their changepoint algorithms, not evidence of trading PnL or a promised speedup here. [PMLR paper page](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/altamirano23a.html); [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.04759)
- **Aleksandr Y. Aravkin, James V. Burke and Gianluigi Pillonetto, “Robust and Trend-Following Student’s t Kalman Smoothers,” SIAM Journal on Control and Optimization 52(5), 2891–2916 (2014), DOI 10.1137/130918861; preprint arXiv:1303.5588 (2013).** Heavy-tailed **measurement** errors make smoothing robust to outliers, while heavy-tailed **process** noise lets the state track sudden changes. Their algorithms are offline MAP smoothers with cost linear in time-series length per iteration. This supports a scalar online process-heavy-tail analogue, not a claim that their offline solver can be dropped into an event callback. [Original arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.5588); [DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1137/130918861)
- **Fulvio Corsi, Davide Pirino and Roberto Renò, “Threshold bipower variation and the impact of jumps on volatility forecasting,” Journal of Econometrics 159(2), 276–288 (2010), DOI 10.1016/j.jeconom.2010.07.008.** Threshold bipower variation combines bipower variation and threshold estimation to separate continuous quadratic variation from discontinuous components and detect jumps. It supports maintaining separate calm-volatility and jump-risk states. Its volatility-forecasting results on financial return series do not establish post-jump direction or PnL here. [Institutional record and accepted manuscript](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/id/eprint/4435/)

### Inferences

- **1. Build a two-channel, two-scale permanent-jump posterior.** Correct each new buy limit with the current buy offset and each new sell limit with the current sell offset; maintain a second, slower estimate from buy-IOC price minus one tick and sell-IOC price plus one tick. Aggregate the limit channel into small fixed-time robust cells so one extreme placement distance X is not a jump. Compare a narrow diffusion/ordinary-offset hypothesis with a broad displaced-level hypothesis, with equal-magnitude positive and negative tails. A fixed small O(1) posterior recursion is enough initially; a capped run-length BOCPD is a second version, not the first. Use weak evidence to cancel/shrink adversely exposed resting orders, but stronger sustained or cross-channel evidence to reset S and take toward it. Once reset, do not expect mean reversion: the jump becomes the new center. **Smallest clean ablation:** add only posterior-triggered S reset/covariance inflation while leaving existing target prices, cross rules and sizing unchanged. Compare it with both no reset and the current post-large-IOC inflation, repeating on matched synthetic jump/no-jump seeds. Only afterward test a detector-triggered directional cross. Report detection delay against true synthetic S, false resets per hour, PnL in the 5 s and 30 s after jumps, ordinary-period PnL, and lost profitable fills caused by false resets. **Why different:** this acts on pre-touch limit-price evidence and a posterior state change, not a post-sweep guard, an OLS prediction, or an inventory markout early-exit rule. **Failure assumptions:** BOCPD normally models a change in data-generating parameters, so a level-jump observation model must be constructed explicitly; the public six-per-hour rate cannot be assumed fixed; requiring both channels can forfeit the early-warning advantage; and a posterior cannot predict an exogenous jump before any evidence arrives. [Adams–MacKay](https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.3742); [Altamirano–Briol–Knoblauch](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/altamirano23a.html)
- **2. Use process-heavy-tail filtering as a fallback, not as a second layer on top of method 1.** A lightweight online approximation to process-noise Student-t filtering is a two-component scalar mixture: a normal Kalman component and a high-process-variance component. Update each with the same innovation, Bayes-reweight their mixture probabilities, and combine their S means. Use one or two scalar IRLS iterations only if this is simpler; do not port the offline smoother. Coherent displacement across independent limit orders should increase the jump component, whereas offset-X noise should not. Apply measurement downweighting only to isolated inconsistent observations—robust measurement noise alone would incorrectly reject the real jump. **Smallest clean ablation:** replace only the current big-IOC covariance-inflation rule with the two-component process-noise update; retain the same S observations, cross threshold, sizing, and order logic. **Why different:** jump adaptation is posterior- and innovation-driven and can occur before an IOC sweep, instead of a deterministic post-large-IOC covariance bump or another fitted return regression. **Failure assumptions:** the cited result is for offline MAP smoothers, so this online mixture is an engineering analogue rather than its proved algorithm; stationary degrees of freedom and Gaussian components are approximations; two means can lag if the mixture collapses; and the method reacts after observations rather than anticipating jumps. [Aravkin–Burke–Pillonetto](https://arxiv.org/abs/1303.5588)
- **3. Split calm volatility from jump exposure in sizing and risk.** On a regular small time grid, maintain decayed sums of squared S increments and adjacent absolute-increment products, plus a separate decayed accumulator for increments classified as jumps. The first two give a TBPV-inspired continuous-vol estimate; the jump accumulator controls tail exposure only. Use calm sigma for ordinary signal normalization and take-sizing, rather than allowing the 30–100 tick permanent level shift to enter the same sigma and suppress all subsequent crosses. Separately compute adverse gap exposure using inventory **plus live resting and in-flight fill exposure**, not just current I. The safe asymmetric sequence under a rising posterior is: cheap cancel first, assume that cancel can lose the 5 ms race, cap residual adverse exposure, and only then take toward the new S at confidence strong enough to pay the sweep cost. **Smallest clean ablation:** replace only the existing volatility input to cross sizing/edge with the continuous estimator for a fixed interval after detected jumps; leave signal, position cap, and all order prices unchanged. Compare post-jump traded volume and PnL as well as full-tape PnL. A separate later experiment can cap exposure by an online upper quantile of signed jump magnitudes; do not bundle that risk cap into the first test. **Why different:** current vol scaling treats level shifts and continuous risk as one quantity; this explicitly preserves directional opportunity while retaining a distinct gap-risk state. **Failure assumptions:** classical TBPV asymptotics assume much more regular high-frequency observations than sparse discrete IOC readings and tick-rounded limit prices, so the implementation is a robust estimator, not a valid asymptotic jump test; permanent S jumps are not transient volatility bursts; bipower scaling constants calibrated on Brownian data should not be treated as exact here; and quantiles from roughly six jumps/hour are extremely noisy. [Corsi–Pirino–Renò](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/id/eprint/4435/)

### Gaps

- No cited source validates a particular posterior threshold, sample interval, jump prior, or PnL gain on this synthetic exchange. Those must be selected on generated jump/no-jump sessions, with the public tape used as confirmation rather than as the sole tuning set.
- The robust changepoint paper’s exact diffusion-score-matching posterior was not re-derived here. The proposed two-component filter is the smallest deployable analogue, not a claim to reproduce that algorithm.
- It remains unresolved how early new limit prices reveal the full jump relative to the visible touch and whether jump duration varies. The local fit explicitly lists these as open measurements. [Local Round-2 generator analysis](../../docs/DATA_ANALYSIS.md#round2_public-same-engine-plus-jumps)

## 2. How should execution, queue survival and mixed passive/aggressive control change?

### Takeaway

Use queue survival to answer **“will this inventory-reducing limit order fill before its fair value is invalidated?”**, and use a small event-triggered mode controller for **“hold, post, or pay the spread now?”**. Do not solve a large HJB or apply a fixed liquidation schedule: the actionable novelty is to price the option to wait against permanent adverse movement and 5 ms execution races.

### Cited Findings

- **Andrew W. Lo, A. Craig MacKinlay and June Zhang, “Econometric Models of Limit-Order Executions,” Journal of Financial Economics 65, 31–71 (2002), DOI 10.1016/S0304-405X(02)00134-4.** They model time-to-first-fill and time-to-completion with survival analysis and include limit price, size, spread and volatility as explanatory variables. Their stated findings include strong sensitivity to limit price and poor quality of hypothetical transaction/first-passage proxies relative to actual executions. This supports explicit competing fill/censoring models; it does not validate their estimated coefficients on another market. [Author’s publication page and abstract](https://web.mit.edu/Alo/www/Papers/limit10.html)
- **Alvaro Arroyo, Alvaro Cartea, Fernando Moreno-Pino and Stefan Zohren, “Deep Attentive Survival Analysis in Limit Order Books: Estimating Fill Probabilities with Convolutional-Transformers” (2023), arXiv:2306.05479.** The paper formulates the passive-versus-aggressive decision around a distribution of fill times conditional on time-varying LOB features and evaluates survival predictions with proper scoring rules. Its convolutional-Transformer/monotonic decoder is undeployable under our constraints, but the relevant non-deep mechanism is distributional, state-dependent fill survival rather than a binary queue guard. [Original arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05479)
- **Fabien Guilbaud and Huyên Pham, “Optimal high-frequency trading with limit and market orders,” Quantitative Finance 13(1), 79–94 (2013), DOI 10.1080/14697688.2012.708779; arXiv:1106.5040 (2011).** Their model combines continuous limit quoting with market orders at discrete dates, inventory control, and a tick-valued spread, formulating mixed regular/impulse control. The abstract also discusses execution-priority and Cox fill-intensity calibration. This supports the maker/taker mode structure, not importing its numerical policy or fill parameters. [Original arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.5040)
- **Alvaro Cartea and Sebastian Jaimungal, “Optimal execution with limit and market orders,” Quantitative Finance 15(8), 1279–1291 (2015), DOI 10.1080/14697688.2015.1032543.** The original abstract says the work develops an optimal execution policy for an investor using limit and market orders. This supports jointly comparing immediate execution with passive waiting; it does not establish that any closed-form quote is suitable for a permanent-impact, jumpy latent-S tape. [Publisher DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1080/14697688.2015.1032543)
- **Baron Law and Frederi Viens, “Market Making under a Weakly Consistent Limit Order Book Model,” arXiv:1903.07222, v1 2019 and v4 2020.** Their marked-point-process model permits arbitrary order volume, price-jump and spread distributions, adds market orders, respects price-time priority and consistent price movements, and uses optimal switching/impulse control. They show numerically that inconsistent LOB dynamics can severely overstate market-making profit. This is the closest conceptual control paper, but its finite-difference HJBQVI is not a lightweight online solution. [Original arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.07222)

### Inferences

- **4. Estimate fill survival with queue evolution and S invalidation as competing risks.** Use “shadow” virtual quotes that do not trade: record queue ahead at insertion, then update that queue from observed trades and individual-ID quantity removals. Maintain decayed discrete-time hazard counts—`fills / shadow-lots-at-risk` and `invalidations / shadow-lots-at-risk`—in a very small number of bins based on price distance, log queue-ahead, signed `S_hat − quote`, spread and jump posterior. Include fills and invalidations of live quotes as outcomes; order cancellation caused by the strategy is not automatically non-informative censoring. For reducing inventory, compare the chance of a passive fill over a short action horizon with the expected cost of an immediate IOC sweep and the cost of adverse S movement while waiting. Use a nonparametric hazard table or online logistic updates, not another ex-post OLS return fit. **Smallest clean ablation:** change **only passive inventory-unwind retention**: wait while the predicted fill probability before invalidation exceeds one predefined action threshold; otherwise use the existing unwind cross. Leave passive entry disabled, and leave directional taking, target inventory and quote prices unchanged. Report time-to-flat, spread/depth paid to flatten, unwind-fill markouts and total PnL. **Why different:** tried queue guards decide whether to enter or retain a quote; this compares the time value of an existing queue position against urgent execution, using forward fill/invalidation survival rather than size threshold, post-sweep flow, or fixed early exit. **Failure assumptions:** proportional hazards are not assumed by the binned version, but rare cells can be badly estimated; queue-ahead reconstruction can be wrong around simultaneous matches; ghost cancel rows must not decrement queues; invalidation is endogenous to S and therefore a competing event; and shadow fills can differ from real race exposure unless the virtual order follows the same 5 ms insertion rule. [Lo–MacKinlay–Zhang](https://web.mit.edu/Alo/www/Papers/limit10.html); [Arroyo et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05479)
- **5. Make crossing a discrete impulse in an explicit evidence state, with hysteresis and acknowledgement barriers.** Maintain only `NORMAL`, `JUMP_PENDING`, and `JUMP_FOLLOW` modes. On a sufficiently strong signed jump posterior, calculate the **actual sweep cost through current book levels**, the residual direct-signal edge after 5 ms, and worst-case fills from resting orders during that latency. Send one bounded IOC toward the existing target, block duplicates until private fill/cancel outcomes return, and require weaker continuing evidence to remain in the mode than to enter it. Cancel/shrink adversely exposed resting orders concurrently but do not count a cancel as successful until confirmed. Return to normal when evidence decays; do not use a fixed seconds-long “storm” timeout because S remains permanently shifted. **Smallest clean ablation:** replace only the behavior after a high jump posterior with this one-IOC, acknowledgement-gated impulse; leave the posterior, ordinary crossing rule, target size and passive rules untouched. Compare against the current fixed-threshold early exit/cross on jump-event PnL, duplicate orders, cancel-race fills, worst inventory and PnL outside jump windows. **Why different:** it is a finite action-value/mode-switching decision based on permanent expected displacement and executable depth, not a resting-entry rule, a generic flow guard, or “exit after loss/time X.” **Failure assumptions:** the one-step comparison is not an optimal HJB policy; book depth can change during 5 ms; posterior edge can overshoot; permanent impact in the generator belongs to market IOCs and observed S, whereas the fixed tape does not endogenously react to our trading; and any expected-resiliency term would wrongly encourage waiting for reversion. [Guilbaud–Pham](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.5040); [Law–Viens](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.07222)
- **Common evaluation discipline for methods 4–5:** use matched synthetic seeds with true jump timestamps, both calibrated-jump and no-jump sessions, and mirrored jump signs. Select on training seeds, check unseen seeds, and only then run the exact finalist file on public Round 2. Alongside PnL, report drawdown and PnL decomposed into directional carry versus spread capture, event-window cross PnL, fill survival calibration, inventory quantiles, breach penalty, rejected/late cancels, and full-book IOC slippage. A variant that only wins because it retained inventory through jumps should not be read as an execution improvement.

### Gaps

- The literature does not provide a closed-form action-value formula using this exchange’s full book, permanent impact, and 5 ms races. Deriving a full HJB policy would be a separate research project and is not recommended as the next step.
- Shadow-fill evaluation must itself be audited. Lo–MacKinlay–Zhang explicitly warn that hypothetical executions can be poor proxies; here they are useful only if the virtual queue faithfully reproduces insertion latency, price-time priority, matches and invalidation. [Source](https://web.mit.edu/Alo/www/Papers/limit10.html)
- No ablation numbers are asserted in this note. Each proposal is a mechanism with a falsifiable experiment, not a predicted PnL improvement.

## 3. What should be gated, rejected, or treated only as practitioner evaluation folklore?

### Takeaway

Marked intensity is useful as a **diagnostic**, not as an assumed edge: the fitted generator has Poisson arrivals and no order-side memory, so full Hawkes/ACD fitting is likely to manufacture regimes. Ready Trader Go repositories add useful evaluation warnings but no verified new strategy mechanism; transient-impact execution schedules and numerical HJB/deep survival models are poor next investments.

### Cited Findings

- **Hai-Chuan Xu and Wei-Xing Zhou, “Modeling aggressive market order placements with Hawkes factor models,” PLOS ONE 15(1), e0226667 (2020), DOI 10.1371/journal.pone.0226667.** They use a bivariate marked Hawkes model with order volume as a mark and compare a difference-of-exponentials kernel with a monotonous exponential. They also use the time-rescaling transform: integrated-intensity durations should be iid unit exponential under a correct point-process model. Their empirical self-/cross-excitation findings concern one Shenzhen stock and are not evidence for this tape. [Open-access original article](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6953867/)
- **Emmanuel Bacry and Jean-François Muzy, “Hawkes model for price and trades high-frequency dynamics,” Quantitative Finance 14(7), 1147–1166 (2014), DOI 10.1080/14697688.2014.897000; arXiv:1301.1135 (2013).** Their four kernels distinguish trade self-excitation, price-change mean reversion, trade impact on price and price-change feedback on activity. That decomposition is useful for asking whether impact or true arrival excitation is being measured; it does not justify assuming all four are present here. [Original arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.1135)
- **Robert F. Engle and Jeffrey R. Russell, “Autoregressive Conditional Duration: A New Model for Irregularly Spaced Transaction Data,” Econometrica 66(5), 1127–1162 (1998), DOI 10.2307/2999632.** ACD models duration dependence directly. The Xu–Zhou methods section explicitly contrasts this indirect duration characterization with Hawkes conditional intensity. That makes ACD a reasonable null-model diagnostic and a poor default strategy feature when durations are already consistent with Poisson arrivals. [Bibliographic record in the PLOS article’s references](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6953867/)
- **Anna A. Obizhaeva and Jiang Wang, “Optimal trading strategy and supply/demand dynamics,” Journal of Financial Markets 16(1), 1–32 (2013), DOI 10.1016/j.finmar.2012.09.001.** This is a foundational LOB execution model of intertemporal supply/demand and execution cost. Its role here is to make the resiliency assumption explicit, not to prescribe order splitting when observed S impact is permanent. [Author-hosted original paper](https://web.mit.edu/wangj/www/pap/ObizhaevaWang13.pdf); [DOI record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.finmar.2012.09.001)
- **Ready Trader Go practitioner evidence is self-reported.** The LiquidBears 2023 repository describes mid/imbalance quoting, short price-change windows for spread, interpreting volume as activity rather than direction, a bug that made the bot idle for half a match, degraded results when other autotraders were introduced, and latency/multi-agent tests as future work. Its “top 128/1050” and PnL statements are claims in the team repository, not independently verified official standings or transferable performance measurements. [Team repository](https://github.com/mglush/ready-trader-go-2023)
- **The official Ready Trader Go rules differ materially from this task**, including message and position constraints and its own matching environment. They are worth reading to avoid porting contest-specific constraints, but not as strategy evidence. [Official how-to-play page](https://readytradergo.optiver.com/how-to-play/)

### Inferences

- **6. Gate marked-intensity work behind a zero-PnL diagnostic; do not put another generic Hawkes/ACD guard into the strategy now.** First compute inter-arrival residual diagnostics separately for raw IOC arrivals, executed IOCs, level-sweeping IOCs and large-sweep events, using current online baseline intensity only. Use time-integrated residuals and autocorrelation/QQ diagnostics rather than accepting a high in-sample likelihood. If no stable excitation survives across time chunks, stop. If a specific executed-sweep stream does show reproducible memory, a two-state exponential marked intensity is enough: marks are executed volume and sweep depth, not raw signed trade count. **Smallest clean Round-2 ablation:** diagnostic only, with no strategy changes. Only after a positive result, replace the existing decayed-flow **arrival-count** component with a decayed **sweep-mark intensity** while keeping its coefficient and all quote logic unchanged. **Why potentially different:** current decayed flow measures recent directional activity and generic Hawkes guards predict bursts; marks measure how far observed flow actually penetrated stale liquidity and can distinguish impact from arrival clustering. **Failure assumptions:** the fitted tape uses independent Poisson limit and IOC arrivals, fair-coin IOC side, and no sequence memory, so true self-excitation should be absent. [Local generator evidence](../../docs/DATA_ANALYSIS.md#the-engine-sample_v2-and-round1) Executed trades remain state-dependent even when raw arrivals are Poisson, which can create spurious “excitation”; fitted Hawkes feedback can therefore rediscover book/S dynamics rather than predict new orders. The 2020 paper’s delayed kernel and Shenzhen findings are not reasons to add parameters without the gate. [Xu–Zhou](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6953867/); [Bacry–Muzy](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.1135)
- **Transferable practitioner lesson: evaluation breadth and failure-state audits, not their quote rules.** The RTG repository’s idle-bot failure suggests explicitly testing that every evidence/mode state has a valid route back to normal and logging time spent unable to act. Its self-reported open-market versus multi-autotrader gap suggests evaluating the mechanism outside one favorable environment, but our tape is fixed and non-reactive, so opponent-style tuning and multi-agent exploit evaluation do not transfer. The useful stress set here is jump/no-jump seeds, sign mirroring, altered jump rate/size, continuous volatility and IOC impact—not importing RTG spreads, hedging, message caps, or volume/mid heuristics. Treat its claimed rank and dollar figures as unverified anecdote. [LiquidBears repository](https://github.com/mglush/ready-trader-go-2023)
- **Lower-value or not applicable methods; avoid spending the next iteration on them:**
  - **Almgren–Chriss-style fixed schedules or Obizhaeva–Wang transient-resilience splitting.** There is no exogenous parent quantity/deadline here, alpha is short-lived, and local analysis finds IOC impact on S permanent. Splitting to wait for transient recovery attacks the wrong cost model. Any liquidation urgency should come from changing S, inventory risk and queue survival instead. Obizhaeva–Wang is valuable for identifying that rejected assumption. [Original paper](https://web.mit.edu/wangj/www/pap/ObizhaevaWang13.pdf); [local permanent-impact fit](../../docs/DATA_ANALYSIS.md#synthetic-twin-of-round1)
  - **Full finite-difference HJB/HJBQVI solvers.** Law–Viens and Guilbaud–Pham justify mixed impulse control conceptually, but discretizing S, spread, inventory, queue state and jump posterior is neither lightweight nor robust to changing generator parameters. Reuse the discrete action decomposition, not the numerical solver. [Law–Viens](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.07222)
  - **The convolutional-Transformer survival model, particle filters, deep LOB nets and RL execution.** They require stored learned weights or heavy online simulation. The survival **target** and two-hypothesis Bayesian **state** are transferable without those estimators. [Arroyo et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05479)
  - **Full multivariate Hawkes or ACD fitting by default.** The generator’s documented Poisson streams make this a misspecified source of false regime alerts. Diagnose residual memory first; do not infer trading value from likelihood improvements on another market. [Local model](../../docs/DATA_ANALYSIS.md#the-engine-sample_v2-and-round1); [Xu–Zhou diagnostic](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6953867/)
  - **EVT-only jump predictors, persistent post-jump “storm” regimes, and blanket tighter inventory limits after any large return.** None can forecast an exogenous Poisson jump before evidence; permanent level shifts do not imply the same subsequent arrival intensity; and blanket de-risking can discard exactly the jump-following edge. Estimate continuous risk, current posterior and executable gap exposure separately. [Jump-separation motivation](https://openaccess.city.ac.uk/id/eprint/4435/)
  - **More microprice/OFI/queue-threshold tuned regressions or another post-sweep guard.** They repeat mechanisms already covered and tried locally, whereas the largest unexploited source distinction is coherent new-limit-price movement before the touch.
  - **Contest-specific cross-instrument hedging, recurring bot/T+1 exploits, participant-ID folklore, or unverified rankings/profits.** One instrument and a fixed synthetic tape remove their mechanism. Newer IMC material already catalogued in `RESEARCH.md` is intentionally not repeated here.

### Gaps

- No official independent Ready Trader Go ranking was retrieved for the cited team repository. Its rank, PnL and causal explanations should therefore remain labelled self-reported. The repository’s proposed future latency tests are not completed empirical evidence.
- No external source establishes whether executed-IOC intensity on this generator has residual serial dependence after conditioning on book state and S. That is precisely why the proposed Hawkes/ACD step must begin as a diagnostic.
- This pass did not find a serious open-source HFT implementation with a verified, materially different, deployable mechanism beyond the already-covered queue simulator/latency work. Generic exchange-framework and crypto-bot architecture is not a substitute for an edge mechanism.
